My Partner's: Hadeel (Heatmap)
1. Which KPI did you choose and why?
I chose Revenue Distribution by Category and City because it provides a granular view of how different geographic locations contribute to specific product sectors. This KPI is essential for understanding regional demand and optimizing localized marketing strategies.

2. Why did you choose this chart type?
I used a Heatmap because it is the most effective way to visualize cross-categorical data (City vs. Category). The color intensity allows stakeholders to instantly spot "hotspots" of high revenue without reading individual numbers, making complex data easy to digest.

3. What insight did you find?
The heatmap revealed that while Electronics and Clothing are top performers across the board, their dominance is heavily concentrated in specific cities. This suggests that consumer preferences in the Amman Digital Market are not uniform and vary significantly by location.
